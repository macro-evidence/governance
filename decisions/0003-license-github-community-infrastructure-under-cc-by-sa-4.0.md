# 0003. License .github community infrastructure under CC BY-SA 4.0

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-21

## Context

The `macro-evidence/.github` repository provides Macro Evidence's public organization profile and organization-wide default community health infrastructure, including contribution guidance, security reporting guidance, pull request and issue templates, and contributor conduct policy.

The repository has been published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0). That license permits reuse, adaptation, redistribution, and commercial use subject to its license conditions, but does not require shared adaptations to remain under reciprocal licensing terms.

Macro Evidence reviewed whether this repository should remain maximally permissive or operate as a reciprocal open-content commons. The review considered the repository's purpose, downstream-user clarity, current contribution history, third-party provenance, commercial reuse, licensing simplicity, and Macro Evidence's broader separation between rights in open work and rights in organizational identity.

The repository is primarily community and participation infrastructure rather than product software. Its substantive materials define how people encounter, contribute to, report problems to, and participate in Macro Evidence. Small declarative GitHub configuration files also exist in the repository, but no material software codebase has been identified that would independently justify a software-specific repository license.

Repository history reviewed for this decision did not identify external substantive contributions requiring separate relicensing permission. The current Code of Conduct incorporates third-party Contributor Covenant material and is handled separately rather than being silently relicensed by this decision.

Existing CC BY 4.0 grants remain valid under their terms. This decision does not revoke or retroactively replace those grants.

## Decision

Beginning with implementation of this decision, publish the maintained `macro-evidence/.github` repository and future revisions under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Macro Evidence treats its organization-wide community and participation infrastructure as a reciprocal open-content commons. The material remains available for use, redistribution, adaptation, and commercial reuse, while adaptations that are shared must comply with the ShareAlike requirements of CC BY-SA 4.0 or an applicable compatible license.

The repository's committed `LICENSE` remains the canonical license text for the current repository state.

Macro Evidence's trademarks, names, marks, visual identity, official identifiers, and official status remain governed separately by the Trademarks Policy. Rights granted by the repository license do not themselves grant permission to present a fork, adaptation, service, or other use as official Macro Evidence.

This decision applies only to `macro-evidence/.github`. It does not change the licenses of `macro-evidence/governance`, `macro-evidence/website`, `macro-evidence/macro-data-observatory`, data obtained or produced through Macro Data Observatory, or Macro Evidence's trademark and identity rights.

## Consequences

- The `.github` repository moves from CC BY 4.0 to CC BY-SA 4.0 for its maintained current and future releases.
- Commercial use, redistribution, and adaptation remain permitted under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Shared adaptations are subject to ShareAlike requirements.
- Downstream users receive one repository-wide content-licensing model rather than needing different default licenses for individual community-health materials.
- Historical material already made available under CC BY 4.0 remains usable under those previously granted terms.
- Third-party material is not relicensed merely by changing the repository default; its applicable upstream terms must continue to be respected.
- The organization-wide Code of Conduct remains a separate policy decision and may be revised or replaced independently of this licensing decision.
- The website, MDO, governance, data, and trademark licensing questions remain governed by their own existing decisions and policies.
