# Macro Evidence Entity Contributor License Agreement

> Version 1.0.0 · Active · Public reference form; not itself an executed agreement · Last updated 2026-09-24

---

Thank you for your interest in contributing to Macro Evidence projects.

For this Agreement, **Macro Evidence** is the project identity under which covered repositories and materials are maintained. **We**, **Us**, and **Our** mean **Siddharth Chauhan**, as the present contracting party and recipient of the rights granted under this Agreement, and any permitted successor or assignee to the extent rights and obligations validly pass under Section 6.3 or by operation of law. Macro Evidence is not represented by this Agreement as a separately incorporated legal person.

This Entity Contributor License Agreement (the **Agreement**) documents rights granted by a legal entity whose rights are implicated by Contributions to Macro Evidence. It may cover more than one Macro Evidence project. It supplements the Individual Contributor License Agreement required by the Macro Evidence contribution process for natural persons who Submit Contributions. To make this Agreement effective, execute the private signing copy using the published Macro Evidence contributor-agreement process. This is a legally binding document; read it carefully before agreeing to it.

## 1. Definitions

**You** and **Your** mean the Legal Entity executing this Agreement.

**Legal Entity** means an entity that is not a natural person. **Affiliate** means another Legal Entity that controls, is controlled by, or is under common control with You. For this definition, **control** means (i) the direct or indirect power to direct management, whether by contract or otherwise, (ii) ownership of fifty percent (50%) or more of securities entitled to elect management or equivalent governing persons, or (iii) beneficial ownership sufficient to exercise that control.

**Covered Affiliate** means an Affiliate expressly identified in the private Entity Coverage Schedule for which You represent that You have authority to make the grants and representations in this Agreement. An Affiliate is not covered merely because it satisfies the control definition.

**Authorized Contributor** means a natural person identified in the private Entity Coverage Schedule as authorized to Submit Contributions on Your behalf or on behalf of a Covered Affiliate.

**Authenticated Contribution Identity** means a public contribution account, such as a GitHub account, identified in the private Entity Coverage Schedule and bound to an Authorized Contributor using the published account-binding process. It is administrative evidence of account control; it is not conclusive proof that every activity performed through the account was personally authorized by that person.

**Covered Project** means a Macro Evidence repository or project that expressly states that this Agreement applies to covered external contributions.

**Contribution** means any work of authorship intentionally Submitted by an Authorized Contributor on Your behalf for inclusion in or documentation of a Covered Project and for which You or a Covered Affiliate own, control, or have the legal authority to license the rights required by this Agreement. If You do not control all rights needed for any part of a proposed Contribution, the Authorized Contributor and You must follow Section 3 and the published Third-Party Material Process.

**Copyright** means copyright, neighboring rights, and other rights protecting works of authorship that You or a Covered Affiliate own, control, or have the right to license, for the full duration of those rights and any lawful extensions or renewals.

**Database Rights** means any sui generis database right or analogous right protecting a database or collection of data, separate from Copyright, that You or a Covered Affiliate own, control, or have the right to license in connection with a Contribution.

**Material** means the work of authorship made available by Us to third parties through the Covered Project to which the Contribution was Submitted. After a Contribution is Submitted, it may be included in the Material.

**Submit** means any electronic or written communication sent by an Authorized Contributor to Us or to persons acting on Our behalf for the purpose of discussing or improving Material, including communications through source-control systems, issue trackers, code-review systems, and mailing or messaging systems managed by or for a Covered Project. Another form of communication is treated as a Submission only if it is captured in a durable project record that identifies the submitted work and the submitter. A communication is excluded as **Not a Contribution** only when it is conspicuously designated that way in writing at the time it is sent. A later designation does not retroactively withdraw rights granted for an earlier Submission. Material previously excluded as Not a Contribution becomes a Contribution only if an Authorized Contributor deliberately re-submits it for inclusion under this Agreement in a durable project record.

**Submission Date** means the date on which an Authorized Contributor Submits a Contribution on Your behalf.

**Coverage Start Date** means the earliest date from which a pre-Effective-Date Contribution may be eligible for coverage under Section 2.8, as stated in the private Entity Coverage Schedule for the relevant Authorized Contributor or, if the Schedule states one entity-wide date, that date. A Coverage Start Date before the Effective Date does not by itself identify or license a pre-Effective-Date Submission. If no Coverage Start Date is stated, it is the Effective Date.

**Effective Date** means the date on which both You and Us have executed this Agreement.

Each Contribution covered by this Agreement is identified by the Covered Project, the Authorized Contributor, and the corresponding submission record, such as the pull request, commit, patch, attachment, issue submission, or other recorded communication through which the work was Submitted. The rights granted under this Agreement do not lapse merely because We do not exercise a particular granted right within one year or within any other period of non-use. We may exercise the granted rights at any time during their stated duration.

A Contribution Submitted before the Effective Date becomes licensed under this Agreement on the Effective Date only if (a) it was Submitted on or after the applicable Coverage Start Date and (b) the private Entity Coverage Schedule specifically identifies that Submission as covered by Authorized Contributor, repository or Covered Project, and a durable Submission identifier, either directly or through an attached schedule incorporated into that record. A pre-Effective-Date Submission not so identified is not covered retroactively by this Agreement. For a Contribution that does not yet exist on the Effective Date, the applicable license takes effect when the work comes into existence and is Submitted on or after the Effective Date.

## Schedule A - Contributor Responsibility and Risk Allocation

Schedule A is part of this Agreement.

### A.1 Independent Relationship; No Authority to Bind

You and each Authorized Contributor act independently of Us. Nothing in this Agreement, the submission or acceptance of a Contribution, repository access, attribution, review activity, or community participation creates employment, agency, partnership, joint venture, fiduciary status, sponsorship entitlement, or authority for You or an Authorized Contributor to act for or bind Us.

Neither You nor an Authorized Contributor may represent that You or they speak for Macro Evidence, make commitments on Our behalf, incur obligations in Our name, or use Macro Evidence names, marks, or visual identity in a manner implying authorization or endorsement unless separately authorized in writing.

### A.2 Responsibility Follows the Actor

Each party remains responsible for its own independent acts, omissions, statements, commitments, and misconduct. Our acceptance, review, publication, distribution, licensing, or other authorized use of a Contribution does not make Us responsible for Your independent conduct or for statements, commitments, or representations made by You or an Authorized Contributor without Our written authority.

Nothing in this Section limits responsibility that applicable law independently imposes on a party for that party's own conduct.

### A.3 Entity Indemnity

To the maximum extent permitted by law, You will indemnify and hold Us and any permitted assignee under Section 6.3 harmless from third-party claims, proceedings, judgments, damages, settlements, and reasonable documented legal or defense costs to the extent arising from:

(a) Your material breach of Section 3;

(b) a knowing Submission on Your behalf of material that You or the relevant Authorized Contributor did not have authority to Submit or license as represented;

(c) fraud, willful misconduct, or intentional unlawful conduct by You or an Authorized Contributor acting on Your behalf in connection with a Contribution or this Agreement;

(d) a knowing Submission on Your behalf of intentionally malicious functionality, undisclosed credentials or secrets, confidential or trade-secret information, or personal data that You or the relevant Authorized Contributor were not authorized to disclose; or

(e) an unauthorized representation by You or an Authorized Contributor acting on Your behalf that You or they act for, speak for, or may bind Us or Macro Evidence.

Where applicable law permits indemnification of a civil regulatory charge, penalty, or analogous amount arising directly from conduct described above, that amount is included to the extent legally indemnifiable.

This indemnity does not apply to the extent a claim is caused by Our independent modification, combination, representation, deployment, or use of a Contribution outside the rights granted by this Agreement, or by Our own fraud, gross negligence, willful misconduct, or intentional unlawful conduct.

### A.4 Direct Remediation Costs

If Your material breach of Section 3 or conduct described in Section A.3 causes Us to incur reasonable, documented, and reasonably foreseeable direct costs to investigate, contain, remove, replace, or remediate the affected Contribution or its effects, You will reimburse those costs to the extent permitted by law. Covered direct costs may include necessary provenance investigation, credential rotation, incident response, takedown or replacement work, and legally required notifications. This Section does not permit double recovery of an amount already recovered under Section A.3 or another remedy.

### A.5 Defense and Settlement of Covered Claims

We will give You reasonably prompt written notice of a claim for which We seek indemnity under Section A.3. Delay in notice reduces Your obligations only to the extent the delay materially prejudices Your ability to respond.

At Our written request, You will assume the defense of a covered claim using competent counsel reasonably acceptable to Us. We may participate with counsel of Our choosing at Our own expense, except that reasonable documented costs caused by an actual conflict of interest or by Your failure to assume the defense when required remain subject to Section A.3.

If a material conflict of interest exists, the claim seeks injunctive or other non-monetary relief that could materially affect Macro Evidence or rights in the Material, or the defense is not being conducted with reasonable diligence, We may assume control of the defense. Reasonable documented defense costs remain covered to the extent the claim is otherwise indemnifiable under Section A.3.

You may not settle a covered claim in a manner that admits wrongdoing by Us, imposes a non-monetary obligation on Us, restricts Our rights in the Material, or requires payment by Us without Our prior written consent, which will not be unreasonably withheld for a settlement that fully releases Us and is otherwise consistent with this Agreement.

### A.6 Responsibility Boundary

Sections A.3 through A.5 apply only to the extent the covered loss, claim, or cost is attributable to Your breach, a Contribution Submitted on Your behalf, or conduct by You or an Authorized Contributor acting on Your behalf as described in those Sections. They do not make You responsible for an act that is not attributable to You, Covered Affiliates, or Your Authorized Contributors, and they do not make Us responsible merely because You or an Authorized Contributor participated in or contributed to a Macro Evidence project.

### A.7 No Support Obligation

Unless You separately agree in writing, You have no obligation to provide maintenance, support, warranty service, or continued participation for a Contribution. This does not limit obligations that survive under this Agreement.

### A.8 Records and Privacy

The private execution process may retain Your legal identity and address, signatory identity and authority, private contact information, Covered Affiliate and Authorized Contributor information, Authenticated Contribution Identities and account-binding evidence, agreement variant and version, Coverage Start Dates, Effective Date, a cryptographic identifier of the agreed terms, execution evidence, coverage-status history, and other information reasonably necessary to establish and administer this Agreement.

Executed agreements, signatures, private addresses, private email addresses, the private Entity Coverage Schedule, and the private contributor-agreement register are not published merely because a Contribution is public. Such information may be disclosed with the affected person's consent, as reasonably necessary to administer or enforce this Agreement or establish, exercise, or defend legal rights, or when required by applicable law, court order, or other lawful legal process. ## Private Entity Coverage Schedule

The Entity Coverage Schedule is part of the private execution record and is not published. It records, at minimum, the entity's legal name, jurisdiction of formation or existence, private address, authorized signatory and authority evidence, administrative point of contact, any Covered Affiliates and the basis for Your authority to grant rights for them, and Authorized Contributors associated with Authenticated Contribution Identities such as GitHub accounts. It also records the Coverage Start Date applicable to each Authorized Contributor or an entity-wide Coverage Start Date and specifically identifies any pre-Effective-Date Submissions intended to be covered by Authorized Contributor, repository or Covered Project, and durable Submission identifier. Macro Evidence may require reasonable additional entity-identity or authority evidence where the rights position is materially uncertain.

You may update Authorized Contributors, Coverage Start Dates for future coverage, or the administrative point of contact by a signed written notice accepted by Us without re-executing the substantive Agreement when the Legal Entity, Covered Affiliate rights basis, and substantive agreement terms remain unchanged. Adding a Covered Affiliate requires documented authority sufficient for that Affiliate. Removing an Authorized Contributor or Covered Affiliate affects future Submissions on that party's behalf and does not revoke rights already granted for prior Contributions.
