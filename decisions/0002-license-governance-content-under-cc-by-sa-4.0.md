# 0002. License governance content under CC BY-SA 4.0

**Status:** Accepted
**Date:** 2026-08-21

## Context

The `macro-evidence/governance` repository contains Macro Evidence's public organizational governance, documentation standards, trademark policy, and cross-cutting decision records.

The repository has been published under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), which permits reuse and adaptation, including commercial use, subject to its attribution and other license conditions.

Macro Evidence subsequently reviewed whether its governance corpus should remain maximally permissive or operate as a reciprocal open-content commons. The review considered current repository composition, contribution history, third-party provenance, downstream reuse, licensing simplicity, and the long-term consequences of ShareAlike.

The repository contains governance and documentation material rather than software code. The repository history reviewed for this decision did not identify external substantive contributions or incorporated third-party governance material requiring separate relicensing permission.

Existing CC BY 4.0 grants remain valid under their terms. This decision does not revoke or retroactively replace those grants.

## Decision

Beginning with implementation of this decision, publish the maintained macro-evidence/governance repository and future revisions under Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0).

Macro Evidence adopts reciprocal openness for this governance corpus: the material remains available for use, redistribution, adaptation, and commercial reuse, while adaptations that are shared must comply with the ShareAlike requirements of CC BY-SA 4.0 or an applicable compatible license.

The repository's committed `LICENSE` remains the canonical license text for the current repository state.

This decision applies only to `macro-evidence/governance`. It does not change the licenses of `macro-evidence/.github`, `macro-evidence/website`, `macro-evidence/macro-data-observatory`, data obtained or produced through Macro Data Observatory, or Macro Evidence's trademarks and brand identity. Those rights and repositories remain governed by their own applicable policies and licenses unless separately reconsidered.

## Consequences

- The governance repository moves from CC BY 4.0 to CC BY-SA 4.0 for its current and future releases.
- Shared adaptations of material governed by the new license are subject to ShareAlike requirements.
- Commercial use remains permitted under CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Downstream users have less relicensing flexibility than under CC BY 4.0.
- Historical material already made available under CC BY 4.0 remains usable under those previously granted terms.
- Future repository contributions made under the repository's licensing terms enter a reciprocal open-content environment unless separate terms apply.
- `README.md` and `TRADEMARKS.md` are synchronized with the new repository license.
- The `.github`, website, MDO, data, and trademark licensing questions remain separate and require their own evidence and decisions.
