# Macro Evidence - Contributor Agreement Privacy Notice

> Version 1.0.0 · Active · Last updated 2026-09-24

---

Macro Evidence treats contributor-agreement records as private legal and administrative records. For contributor-agreement version 1.0.0, these records are administered by the present contracting party identified in the applicable agreement forms under the Macro Evidence project identity. This notice describes the information handled for contributor-agreement execution and merge verification.

---

## Information collected

Depending on the agreement and contribution path, Macro Evidence may process:

- legal name and signature;
- residence or entity address supplied for contract administration;
- private email address and other administrative contact information;
- GitHub numeric account ID and current username;
- account-binding challenge metadata, verification date, next revalidation due date, and private verification evidence;
- agreement variant, version, Coverage Start Date, any specifically identified pre-Effective-Date covered Submission identifiers, Effective Date, execution date, canonical agreement-terms hash, issued unsigned execution-file hash, and complete executed-file hash;
- contributor-rights coverage status, status history, and derived opaque coverage-token metadata;
- entity name, signatory authority, point-of-contact information, and Authorized Contributor information where an ECLA applies;
- provenance or rights information supplied to resolve employer, entity, generated-material, or third-party-material questions;
- stamp or execution-formality evidence where applicable; and
- communications needed to administer, verify, enforce, or correct the agreement record.

---

## Why the information is used

Macro Evidence uses this information to:

- execute and administer contributor agreements;
- establish the identity and authority of the relevant contributor or rights holder;
- bind public contribution identities to the relevant private rights record and detect or respond to reported account compromise;
- determine whether a covered pull request may pass the contributor-rights merge gate;
- maintain evidence of the rights granted for accepted Contributions;
- respond to provenance, rights, security, or legal disputes; and
- comply with lawful obligations and requests.

---

## Public information

Macro Evidence does not publish residence or entity addresses, signatures, private email addresses, executed agreement files, private entity coverage schedules, stamp records, or the private agreement register merely because a person contributes.

A public pull request may reveal the contributor's ordinary public GitHub identity and may display a non-sensitive status such as **Contributor rights coverage**. That status does not expose the underlying private agreement, identity-verification, authority, or provenance record and does not by itself disclose which private rights basis produced the result.

---

## Storage and access

Executed agreements, the full contributor-rights register, identity/authority evidence, and account-binding evidence are stored privately with access limited to persons who need the information for agreement administration, legal compliance, security, or dispute handling. Public repositories contain only blank or reference legal documents and non-sensitive automation needed to enforce the merge gate. Derived public or GitHub-hosted coverage data must not contain legal names, private addresses, signatures, private email addresses, executed agreements, or reversible identifiers derived from those private fields.

---

## Retention

Macro Evidence retains executed agreements, document-integrity evidence, and the minimum register information reasonably necessary to establish and exercise rights granted under the agreement, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and preserve provenance for accepted Contributions. Redundant administrative information may be deleted when it is no longer reasonably needed for those purposes.

---

## Disclosure

Private agreement information may be disclosed with the affected person's consent; when reasonably necessary to administer or enforce the agreement or establish, exercise, or defend legal rights; when required by applicable law, court order, or other lawful process; or to service providers or professional advisers that need the information for those purposes and are subject to appropriate confidentiality or security obligations.

Macro Evidence does not make contributor-agreement personal information public merely for convenience or community recognition.

---

## Privacy requests and questions

For a copy of an executed agreement, correction of administrative information, a reported account compromise, or a privacy question concerning contributor-rights records, contact [legal@macro-evidence.com](mailto:legal@macro-evidence.com). Where applicable law gives a person rights concerning access, correction, erasure, objection, grievance, or another aspect of personal-data processing, the same contact is the administrative route for exercising or asking about those rights. Requests are handled subject to applicable law and the need to retain evidence reasonably necessary to establish, exercise, or defend rights already granted or to meet another lawful obligation.

The public contact route does not require a contributor to publish private identity, address, signature, or agreement material. Sensitive supporting information should be exchanged only through the designated private legal process.

## Changelog

### 1.0.0 (2026-09-24)

- Initial version. Establishes the private, administrative treatment of contributor-agreement records: what information is collected for execution and merge verification, why it is used, what remains public, how it is stored and accessed, retention, and disclosure limits.
